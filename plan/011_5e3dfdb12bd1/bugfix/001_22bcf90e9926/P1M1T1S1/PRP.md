## Why

- **Satisfies the literal spec (PRD §9.9.2 G1.4).** The requirement says "The Blueprint AND Builder
  prompts MUST instruct the Coder Agent not to delete a throwaway artifact …". Today only the Builder
  complies; the Blueprint's CRITICAL RULES block enumerates G1.1, G1.2, G1.3, G1.5 — skipping G1.4.
- **Reinforces throwaway-survival into generated PRPs.** The Blueprint (Researcher) constructs PRPs;
  adding the rule here guides the Researcher to surface throwaway-survival in the generated PRP's
  coder instructions / success criteria, not just rely on the Builder's Coder-facing block.
- **Low functional risk, high spec compliance.** The Builder already carries the Coder-facing rule
  and G1.3 already blocks the harmful `test ! -f <throwaway>` gate from being authored, so the §9.9.3
  acceptance criteria remain satisfiable regardless — but this completes the dual-prompt requirement.
- **Scope discipline.** S1 = Blueprint bullet + PROMPTS.md mirror + 1 test. The optional executor
  neutralization test-hardening (P1.M1.T2) and the README/ARCHITECTURE verification (P1.M1.T3) are
  separate subtasks.
